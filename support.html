Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need to create an account to play?

No. Se7en Strike Slots never asks for an email, username, password, or social login. Just install the App and start spinning.

Can I win real money or prizes?

No. All coins in the App are virtual and have no real-world value. They cannot be exchanged for cash, gift cards, or any tangible item.

Is Se7en Strike Slots suitable for children?

No. The App is for users aged 18 and over. Age verification is required on first launch.

Where is my game data stored?

Everything (coins, stats, achievements, settings) is saved privately on your iPhone in the iOS app sandbox. Nothing is uploaded to our servers.

How do I reset my progress?

Open the App, go to Settings → Reset Progress, and confirm. Alternatively, delete and reinstall the App to erase all local data.

How do I claim the daily bonus?

Open the App every day and tap the daily bonus card on the lobby. Consecutive days build your streak — Day 7 grants a 7,777-coin jackpot gift. Missing a day resets the streak to Day 1.

How do achievements and the leaderboard work?

Achievements unlock automatically as you meet specific milestones (e.g., 100 total spins, three Triple Seven wins). The leaderboard ranks you against fictional competitors; climb higher by playing more.

How do I change my profile photo?

Go to Profile → Edit Avatar. You can take a new photo with the camera or pick one from your library. Photos are processed on-device and never uploaded.

Does the App use notifications?

Only if you opt in. We use local notifications on-device to remind you about your daily bonus. You can disable them anytime in iOS Settings.

The App crashes or freezes. What should I do?

Please force-close the App, restart your iPhone, and relaunch. If the issue persists, email us with your iOS version and the steps that triggered the crash.

How do I request a refund?

Se7en Strike Slots is fully free to play and does not offer any in-app purchases. If a charge from "Se7en Strike Slots" appears on your statement, please contact Apple Support directly, as Apple handles all App Store transactions.
