This Privacy Policy describes how Se7en Strike Slots ("we", "our", "the App") handles information when you use our application. By installing or using the App you agree to the practices described below.

1. Summary

Se7en Strike Slots does not require an account, does not collect personal information, does not include third-party advertising or analytics SDKs, and stores all gameplay data locally on your device. Virtual coins have no real-world value.

2. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect, transmit, or sell:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or any contact details.
  • Account credentials — there is no login or registration.
  • Payment or financial information — no real money is involved.
  • Location data, advertising identifiers (IDFA), or device identifiers used for tracking.
  • Behavioural, analytics, or advertising data shared with third parties.

3. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

The App saves the following data only on your device, using the iOS system sandbox. This data never leaves your phone unless you choose to export it:

  • Player profile (display name and optional avatar image chosen by you).
  • Game progress: virtual coin balance, level, XP, daily-streak counter.
  • Statistics: total spins, total wins, biggest single win, scratch record, wheel record.
  • Achievement status and leaderboard (local) entries.
  • App preferences: sound effects, haptics, and notification toggle.

You can erase all locally stored data at any time by deleting and reinstalling the App, or by using the in-app Reset Progress option.

4. Camera and Photo Library

If you choose to set a profile avatar, the App may ask for permission to access the camera or your photo library. Images you select are processed on-device and used solely to display your in-game avatar. We do not upload, transmit, or share your photos with anyone. You can deny this permission and still use the App with a default avatar.

5. Notifications

The App may request permission to send local notifications to remind you about your daily bonus streak. Notifications are generated and scheduled on-device. We do not send push notifications from a remote server. You can disable notifications at any time in iOS Settings.

6. Children

Se7en Strike Slots is intended for adults aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect any information from children. If you believe a child under 18 has installed the App, please delete it from their device and contact us at mohrdonald13122000@gmail.com.

7. Third-Party Services

The App does not embed any third-party analytics, advertising, attribution, or social SDKs. The only external network requests are standard Apple App Store receipt validation events handled by the operating system, which Apple governs under its own privacy policy.

8. Your Rights

Because all data is stored locally on your device, you have full control:

  • Access: view your data inside the App at any time.
  • Erase: use the in-app Reset Progress option, or uninstall the App to remove everything.
  • Export: the App does not transmit personal data, so no export endpoint is required.

9. International Use

Data remains on your device wherever you are in the world. This App is not directed at children and is intended for users aged 18 and above. If local laws grant you additional rights (for example under the GDPR, UK GDPR, CCPA/CPRA, LGPD, or PIPL), those rights apply to the extent any personal data is processed; in practice, the App processes no personal data off-device.
